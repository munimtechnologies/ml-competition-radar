## IEEE CIS 2026 Technical Challenge on Domain-Specific Question Answering with LLMs (DSQA)

- Organisers: DaSCI (University of Granada) with IFMIF-DONES; funded by the IEEE Computational
  Intelligence Society; part of the CIS GPAIS task force.
  Official page: https://cis.ieee.org/activities/technical-activities/2026-technical-challenge-on-domain-specific-question-answering-with-llms
  (it answers automated fetches with HTTP 418, so it was not read directly).
- Task: answer expert questions in two domains (the IFMIF-DONES particle-accelerator facility and AI
  research papers) with retrieval over a blind PDF corpus that includes distractors.
- Prizes (IFMIF-DONES news, 2026-10-05): $9,000 / $5,000 / $3,000 / $1,500 / $1,500 (up to $20,000).
- Dates (same source): opened 28 Sep 2026; registration and team merger until 1 Nov; submissions until
  30 Nov; results 14 Dec. The CIS home page says the deadlines were extended after the hosting platform
  was down for five days in October; the new dates were not readable here.
- Evaluation (IFMIF-DONES news): a hidden 100-question expert benchmark (25 samples public); phase 1
  automatic leaderboard; about 20 teams go on to phase 2 (expert panels and a scientific committee).
- Unofficial (participant repos jaime-sql/ieee-cis-dsqa and wznrenyilizhixin-sudo/ifmif-dones-domain-qa):
  fixed base model OLMo 3.1 32B Instruct, preinstalled; one H100; one hour wall clock for loading,
  indexing and answering; no network; zip of at most 10^9 bytes; pinned packages only (no vLLM, no
  flash-attn); one submission per team per day; teams up to four; phase 1 scored by a hidden LLM
  judge as domain-balanced accuracy; a 4-page IEEE-format method description; phase 2 reportedly
  30% accuracy, 30% expert quality, 40% innovation.
- Starter kit: github.com/jucls/IEEE-CIS-DSQA-Starter-Kit (MIT): zero-shot and RAG baselines
  (Docling layout model + MiniLM embeddings), entry point `answer(question, corpus_dir, model_id)`.
- Our read: a good technical fit (our NIH SPARK work is CPU retrieval for biomedical QA), but the
  base model cannot be run on our hardware, so every test would cost one of the daily leaderboard
  submissions, and the original registration deadline (1 Nov) has passed. Worth entering only if the
  extended registration is still open. Watch for a 2027 edition.
