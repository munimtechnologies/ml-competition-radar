## Dates (all 23:59 UTC)

The organisers' timeline page (cis.taskforce.ieee.org/gpais/tc-domain-qa-llms/timeline/), the top box
of the IEEE CIS page, and the Official Rules PDF agree:

- Entry and team merger deadline: **15 November 2026** (was 1 November)
- Final submission deadline: **5 December 2026** (was 30 November)
- Phase 1 ranking and start of expert evaluation: 10 December 2026
- Final ranking: 18 December 2026

The reason given is that the hosting platform was down for five days in October. A second, older
list further down the IEEE CIS page shows different "new" dates that look shifted by one row (start
15 November, entry 5 December, submission 18 December, ranking 14 December); we read it as an
editing slip, since the timeline page and the rules PDF match the top box.

## Eligibility, as written

- The rules PDF: entrants are individuals, or teams of individuals registered on the platform as an
  organisation (at most four members, per the Join page), aged 18 or over; residents of the USA and
  other countries where permitted by law. Each person registers individually with a form, using the
  same email as their Codabench account.
- Its first sentence reads "Contest is open to employees of the Sponsor except employees of IEEE
  Technical Activities operating unit." Read literally it would limit entry to IEEE employees; it is
  probably meant the other way round. Anyone planning to enter should ask the organisers.
- No rule found on AI-assisted or AI-written code. The rules say an entry must be the entrant's
  "sole original work"; public external data is allowed if cited; attacking or jailbreaking the LLM
  evaluator disqualifies.
